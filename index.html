How an interview with Xiya works

  1. 01Candidate joins
  2. 02Integrity checks
  3. 03 · modelXiya interviews
  4. 04Rubric scoring

Xiya introduces the role in the candidate's own language and records consent before anything is shared, then asks its own follow-ups, scores against a rubric and explains its recommendation. The decision stays with a person.

Models in production

In-house

Xiya

The interview model: follow-up questions, rubric scoring and fair pacing, with a focus on non-Western-English speech. Runs the full interview in 10 languages; people make the call.

Open weights · 4B · Apache-2.0

Chittu Guard ↗

Fine-tuned from Qwen3-4B with MLX on 5,250 synthetic examples. Escalates with four tokens instead of writing crisis replies itself.

Open weights · 26M · Apache-2.0

Chittu Story ↗

Four habits that took Tulifo from a blank repo to six products and two open-weights models.

01 · Forward deployed

Embedded with the customer, accountable for production.

I do my best work inside the customer's meetings, codebase and constraints — turning a fuzzy problem into software that runs.

Proof — Tulifo, blank repo to production in three months; fintech and mobility platforms for MoneySmart and Spin.

02 · Agentic AI

Agents that run the whole workflow, not a chat window.

An agent earns its place when it carries a real process end to end — and hands the decision to a person.

Proof — Xiya runs the full interview on Python, FastAPI, LiveKit and the Claude Agent SDK; people make the call.

03 · Retrieval

Answers grounded in your data, not the model's memory.

Retrieval is what makes a model useful on a company's own information — so it gets the same care as the model itself.

Proof — Tulifo's matching and verified pool run on real candidate and job data, over PostgreSQL and Redis.

04 · LLMs in production

Models chosen, trained and shipped like any other system.

Model choice, training and cost are engineering decisions — owned, measured and documented.

Proof — Chittu Guard fine-tuned with MLX, Chittu Story trained from scratch; every release ships with a model card listing its evaluations and known failures.
